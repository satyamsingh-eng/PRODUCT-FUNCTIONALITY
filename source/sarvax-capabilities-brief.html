01 · Relationship intelligence

Pre-Meeting Analysis Agent

Catalogue record

Use case

Advisors walk into client meetings under-prepared—buried in back-to-back calls with no time to review email history, CRM notes, or what was discussed last time. This agent assembles a client-specific briefing before every scheduled call, triggered by the client's name.

How it works

  • Scans prior email conversations and CRM records for the client.
  • Reviews past meeting transcripts and call summaries.
  • Detects ongoing financial discussions, concerns, and priorities.
  • Identifies pending action items and open opportunities.
  • Compiles everything into a structured pre-meeting brief.

Output

  • A pre-meeting dashboard with talking points and relevant context.
  • Relationship insights: sentiment, engagement patterns, and priority interests.
  • A list of pending action items and open items to raise.
  • Recommended collateral to bring into the meeting.
02 · Relationship intelligence

Post-Meeting Analysis Agent

Catalogue record

Use case

After a client call ends, advisors lose time manually updating the CRM, drafting follow-up emails, and trying to remember every commitment made mid-conversation. The agent turns the recorded conversation into a structured record the moment the call ends, ready for advisor sign-off instead of end-of-week cleanup.

How it works

  • Processes the meeting transcript as soon as the call ends.
  • Extracts key financial discussions, decisions, and next steps.
  • Detects specific commitments and action items made during the call.
  • Prepares a Mission Control Dashboard for advisor approval before anything is saved.

Output

  • Draft CRM updates: meeting notes, status changes, and new opportunities.
  • A personalized client follow-up email, ready to send.
  • An internal action checklist with suggested follow-up timelines.
  • Everything routes through advisor approval before it is recorded or sent.
03 · Operations & pipeline

Pipeline & Escalation Tracker

Catalogue record

Use case

Advisors lose track of where each client relationship or case stands because pipeline status is scattered across email threads, calendar invites, and tracker sheets nobody keeps current in real time. This agent maintains a live picture of where every case actually stands without manual updating after each email or call.

How it works

  • Reconciles email and calendar activity against tracker sheets.
  • Reconstructs current pipeline status and turnaround time (TAT) per client or case.
  • Surfaces bottlenecks and stalled items automatically.
  • Proactively manages follow-ups and escalations.

Output

  • An updated, accurate pipeline tracker.
  • A bottleneck and TAT report.
  • A proactive escalation list with recommended next steps.
04 · Compliance & onboarding

KYC Intelligence Officer

Catalogue record

Use case

Onboarding files often contain contradictions or hidden risks that go unnoticed under time pressure, with no consistent way to prioritize which cases need urgent compliance attention. This agent reads a file the way a senior investigator would—looking for what does not add up across documents, not just whether each document individually looks complete.

How it works

  • Investigates onboarding submissions in full.
  • Cross-references documents across the file for contradictions.
  • Correlates hidden risk signals that a single-document review would miss.
  • Ranks open cases by risk and escalates high-risk ones directly to the correct manager.

Output

  • An executive-grade, interactive risk dossier per case.
  • A risk-ranked case queue.
  • Proactive escalation flags for high-risk onboarding files.

How the four records connect across the firm

Preparation creates context. Conversation creates commitments. Pipeline control keeps the commitment moving. Onboarding review protects the firm before a case advances.
